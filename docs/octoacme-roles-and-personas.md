# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation for projects. They collaborate with product and engineering teams to ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans for each feature or release
- Define acceptance criteria validation approach with Product Manager and team
- Execute manual testing and coordinate automated test coverage
- Participate in sprint planning to estimate testing effort
- Track and triage defects; prioritize fixes with the team
- Conduct smoke tests and verify deployments before production release
- Provide quality metrics and test coverage reports

### Goals
- Ensure every feature meets acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Identify issues early to reduce production defects
- Enable confidence in releases through thorough, efficient testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement meetings
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria sign-off
- Daily standups for blocker triage
- Pre-release verification and smoke test reports

---

## Stakeholder/Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and approval authority for projects. They ensure projects deliver on business objectives and have the resources and priority needed to succeed.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and define success metrics with Product Manager
- Approve project charters and major scope changes
- Attend kickoff and milestone reviews
- Escalate and resolve priority conflicts
- Communicate project status and outcomes to executive leadership
- Ensure resources and budget are available

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with business strategy
- Minimize scope creep and maintain clear priorities
- Achieve business outcomes and ROI targets

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval gates
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Ad-hoc escalations for priority or resource decisions
- Executive briefings and release announcements

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical decisions, design reviews, and system integration to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure solutions align with system architecture and standards
- Mentor developers and provide technical guidance
- Coordinate integration across teams and services
- Participate in planning to estimate technical complexity

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and maintain code quality
- Enable team growth through mentorship and guidance

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review meetings
- Sprint planning and refinement
- Code review discussions
- Architecture and dependency coordination across teams

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process impediments, and help teams continuously improve. They ensure the team follows agile practices and maintains a sustainable pace.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Help remove blockers and impediments raised by the team
- Coach the team on agile practices and ceremonies
- Maintain and communicate team capacity and velocity metrics
- Track action items from retrospectives and follow up on improvements
- Shield the team from external distractions and context switching

### Goals
- Keep the team focused and moving efficiently
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Maintain predictable delivery pace and quality

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups (15 min)
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Weekly team sync for impediment review
- One-on-ones with team members to support growth

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that security and regulatory compliance requirements are integrated throughout the project lifecycle, from planning through release and operations.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements during planning
- Review designs and code for security vulnerabilities
- Ensure security scanning is configured in CI/CD pipelines
- Coordinate security reviews and compliance certifications
- Participate in incident response and post-incident reviews
- Communicate security incidents to appropriate stakeholders per policy
- Track security action items and remediation efforts

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Build security into the development process early
- Maintain stakeholder confidence through transparent security practices

### Typical Communication
- Planning phase for requirements gathering
- Design reviews and code reviews for security assessment
- Weekly risk reviews and incident coordination
- Compliance reporting and audit support
